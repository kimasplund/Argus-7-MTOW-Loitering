## Rulings

Ruling: **P2 — tail/boom datum undefined.** `build_tail` places the tail at absolute x=`arm_m`=3.2 while `build_aircraft` puts the wing root LE at x=0.748, so the 3.2 m arm is being measured from the nose rather than from the wing. Correct station is wing-AC + arm = 4.094 m, and the plan's booms (spanning −0.80…2.40) do not reach it — the tail would float unattached. Decided: tail arm is measured wing-AC→tail-AC (standard convention); define `x_wing_le = 0.22·L`, `x_ac = x_wing_le + mac_y·tan(sweep) + 0.25·MAC`, `x_tail_qc = x_ac + arm_m`. — Why: it is the only reading under which the report's 3.2 m arm and its V_h are the same quantity. — Cost if wrong: tail longitudinal station shifts ~0.9 m, changing V_h and CG; caught immediately by the P2 tests and cheap to re-derive.

Ruling: **P3 — boom length came from the defective SCAD, not the report.** Report §2 states no boom length; the 3.2 m in the plan was transcribed from `model/argus7_model.scad`, which is the artifact this whole phase exists to replace. Required length to span wing→tail is 3.646 m. Decided: booms become **derived** — from 0.15 m ahead of the wing root LE to 0.15 m aft of `x_tail_qc` — and `booms.length_m` is demoted to an emitted/asserted value, never a driver. — Why: no parameter may be sourced from a known-defective artifact. — Cost if wrong: boom mass/drag estimate shifts ~14%; flows into P2 aero and is re-derivable from geometry.

Ruling: **P4 — twist sign inverted.** `scale_airfoil` as specified uses zr = xc·sin(t)+zc·cos(t); with `twist_tip_deg=-3.0` this drops the trailing edge, which is nose-UP (washin), the opposite of the documented "negative = washout". Decided: negate the rotation (xr = xc·cos t + zc·sin t, zr = −xc·sin t + zc·cos t) so negative twist gives washout, and add a test asserting the tip section's TE sits above its LE. — Why: washin at the tip reverses the stall-progression intent and inverts the Prandtl bell spanload the design pack calls for. — Cost if wrong: tip loading and stall behaviour inverted — high consequence, which is why it gets an explicit test rather than a comment.

Ruling: **P5 — OpenSCAD section orientation.** The emitted `section` module draws the airfoil in the XY plane and extrudes along Z, so hulling consecutive stations would sweep vertically instead of spanwise. Decided: wrap as `translate([x_le,y,z]) rotate([90,0,0]) linear_extrude(...) rotate([0,0,twist]) polygon(...)` so the section lies in XZ and the hull runs along Y. — Why: the OpenSCAD export must render the same aircraft the STEP does. — Cost if wrong: viewer file is visibly wrong; caught by the Task 6 render.

Ruling: **P1/P6/P7 — implementation latitude.** Implementers add only the imports they use, and may realise `build_fuselage` with any build123d construct that satisfies its tests (lofted circular stations preferred; the plan's `make_face(pl.location * Circle(r).wire())` is unverified API). — Why: the tests, not the illustrative code, are the contract. — Cost if wrong: none; tests gate it.

Note (not a ruling, carry to P2): inverted-V tail tips sit ~0.61 m below the boom line, below the fuselage keel. With no landing gear (parachute recovery), check launch/ground clearance during P2.

## Materials & manufacturing research — landed (research/materials_pack.md, 94 KB, 31 sources)

Key results, sponsor's rod-tube-and-print premise evaluated:
- RIGHT about spar caps (pultruded UD: 1682 MPa compression, 134 GPa, E/rho 87.9 vs 77.8 hand wet layup) and booms (stiffness-critical, 4x strength margin). Break-even on printed ribs (4.5x mass penalty but rib stress 0.06 MPa, 170x below creep floor).
- WRONG about the wing spar (round tube costs exactly 2.07x the material of a cap-and-web spar; ratio = 2h/D, pure geometry) and about skins (printed: +-1.14 mm MJF tolerance vs 0.512 mm step budget, +18.7 kg; film: fails not on streamwise waviness but because it cannot form the leading edge, and its scalloping is a SPANWISE disturbance for which NASA states no criteria exist).
- Surface bar is ~6x LOOSER than assumed: Carmichael allowable h/lambda 0.031-0.044 single-wave at Re 0.6-1.1M, since allowable waviness scales Re^-0.75. NASA TP-2256 measured moldless homebuilt VariEze/Long-EZ wings at h/lambda 0.0030-0.0046 — passing with 2.6-4.9x margin. A garage build CAN hold laminar flow. Measured, not asserted.
- Endurance penalty of the film wing is 16.6-23.3 h (0.69-0.97 d), not the report's implied ~7 h: drag -5.7..-12.4 h, measured dCLmax -0.2 roughness penalty -3.8 h (loiter is stall-constrained so it goes straight into speed), mass +4.9 kg -7.3 h. **Mass-to-endurance exchange rate 1.5 h/kg.**
- Mass closure: recommended build 28.9 kg (closes, 3.6 kg spare); sponsor's full preference 37.4 kg (fails by 4.9); printed skins 51.2 kg (fails by 18.7).

Three findings beyond tasking:
1. Report's spar-cap mass reverse-engineers to a CONSTANT root section run root-to-tip. Properly tapered at its own allowables = 3.8 kg, not 8.6-11.5. ~5.9 kg unclaimed — the entire reason heavier build methods come close.
2. Wing is STIFFNESS-critical, not strength-critical: 14.2% semi-span tip deflection at limit load. Inverts the material argument — figure of merit is E/rho, so pultruded rod's 1682 MPa buys nothing; high-modulus pultrusion (+71% EI at equal mass) is the right lever.
3. **THE WING CANNOT HOLD THE FUEL.** Pack: 160 L gross, 56-72 L usable vs 120 L required.

Controller independently verified finding 3 and got a WORSE result: gross wing internal volume 143.0 L (= k*t/c*S*MAC with measured k=0.6062, t/c=0.1371). Realistic tank (50% chord between spars x 80% span x 88% net of structure) = 50.3 L. Report requires 101.5 kg of fuel = 120 L at its own implied density, 130 L at 0.78 kg/L. **Shortfall 58-61%.** This is design-breaking: report section 2 puts fuel in wing tanks "at the AC" specifically so CG travel stays under 0.5% MAC as fuel burns. Relocating 101.5 kg (40.6% of MTOW) to the fuselage makes CG travel a first-order stability problem, not a detail. Escalating to sponsor rather than ruling — this changes the aircraft, not the code.
